## Synthesis requirement

After individual runs, perform one cross-report synthesis that:

- resolves terminology;
- identifies contradictory assumptions;
- maps recommendations to MagnaMath schemas and modules;
- separates implementable findings from experiments;
- updates evidence tiers;
- produces one prioritized architecture decision record.
