# MagnaMath intent

## Direction

Turn exact polyhedron mathematics into an interactive, visually clear laboratory that connects
screen-based exploration, printable nets, rigid-folding instruction, and real magnetic-tile builds
without overstating geometric, mechanical, or educational evidence.

## Active commitment

MagnaMath entered the active portfolio on 2026-08-11 by explicit user request to take over full
development. The imported 0.4.0 handoff is the starting evidence base, not a production-release
claim.

## Near-term outcome

Produce a reproducible production-hardening baseline: dependency lockfile, clean validation and
build, browser/Canvas parity evidence, exact interaction round-trips, keyboard access, and a
tracker-backed P0 plan. Research findings become architecture decisions only after review.

## Human-owned decisions

- licensing and public distribution;
- child/privacy and telemetry policy;
- production deployment and release authority;
- claims about physical safety, structural stability, learning outcomes, or arbitrary unfolding.
