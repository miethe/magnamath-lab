# MagnaMath Deep Research Program

MagnaMath’s remaining hard problems span computational geometry, rigid mechanisms, physical metrology, fabrication, human-computer interaction, learning science, and spatial computing. The repository includes twelve implementation-oriented prompts.

## Existing briefs

1. Magnetic tile metrology and hinge mechanics.
2. Constrained polyhedron-net optimization.
3. Compound face tiling.
4. Structural feasibility and folding physics.
5. Camera and AR build verification.
6. Print production and fabrication.

## Added for 0.4.0

7. Continuous collision detection and folding-path planning.
8. Closed-loop rigid folding and inverse kinematics.
9. Thick-panel hinge offsets and physical closure.
10. Spatial interaction, accessibility, and XR.
11. Learning science for dynamic geometry and physical play.
12. WebGPU, WASM, physics, and large-model scaling.

## Integration rules

Each research output must be converted into:

- an architecture decision;
- schema changes, if any;
- a benchmark or validation protocol;
- a bounded implementation backlog;
- explicit evidence/fidelity labels;
- a list of claims that remain hypotheses.

Research should not directly overwrite exact geometry or physical profile assumptions. New findings enter through versioned adapters and migrations.

## Recommended sequencing

The first synthesis should prioritize physical metrology, continuous collision, closed-loop kinematics, and thick-panel geometry. These determine which product claims are mechanically defensible. Interaction/accessibility and learning-science research should run in parallel so the solver architecture does not dictate an unusable experience. Performance research should evaluate concrete workloads produced by those algorithms rather than optimize speculatively.
